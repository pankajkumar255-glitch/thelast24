Huge Crowds in Mashhad for Burial of Iran's Late Supreme Leader Khamenei

Key facts

  • Khamenei buried in Mashhad amid massive crowds
  • Fighter jet escort accompanied his coffin
  • ~100 people from Karnataka attended funeral in Tehran
  • Death triggered active US-Iran military conflict

Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was laid to rest in Mashhad on Wednesday as vast crowds turned out to pay their respects to Iran's longtime Supreme Leader, whose killing has precipitated the most serious US-Iran military confrontation in decades. A fighter jet escort accompanied his coffin on the journey home, in imagery that underscored both the gravity of the moment and Iran's determination to project strength even amid active hostilities.

The funeral drew mourners from across the world. Notably, around 100 people from Karnataka were reported in attendance at earlier funeral proceedings in Tehran, highlighting the depth of ties between India's Shia Muslim communities and Iran. The scenes in Mashhad were ones of mass grief, with Iranian state media broadcasting the burial to audiences across the country.

Khamenei's death has left a significant power vacuum at the apex of the Iranian state, with the question of succession likely to be contested and consequential. Iran's political and military establishment faces the dual challenge of managing the war with the United States while also navigating an internal transition of historic importance.

For India, the moment carries strategic weight. Iran is a key partner in India's connectivity plans — including the Chabahar port — and a major energy supplier. The identity and ideological orientation of Khamenei's successor will be watched closely in New Delhi.
