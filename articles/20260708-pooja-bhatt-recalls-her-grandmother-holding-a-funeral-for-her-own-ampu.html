Pooja Bhatt recalls her grandmother holding a funeral for her own amputated leg

Key facts

  • Pooja Bhatt's grandmother held a funeral for her own amputated leg
  • Bhatt shared the story in an interview with The Indian Express
  • Family reaction described as bewilderment: 'What are we doing here?'

Pooja Bhatt revealed one of the more extraordinary family stories to emerge from Bollywood circles in some time: her grandmother, she recounted, actually held a funeral for her own amputated leg — a ceremony that left the family bewildered and asking, in Bhatt's words, 'What are we doing here?'

The anecdote emerged in a candid interview with The Indian Express and quickly spread across social media, drawing a mixture of disbelief, laughter, and admiration for the sheer character of Bhatt's grandmother. The story speaks to the kind of fierce, unconventional individuality that has long been associated with the Bhatt family — known for pushing boundaries both on screen and in their personal lives.

Pooja Bhatt has been increasingly open in recent years about her own life journey, including her well-documented personal struggles and her return to acting. Her willingness to share these vivid, unusual family memories adds to a growing public portrait of a woman who has confronted difficulty with frankness and a certain dark wit.

The image of a funeral for an amputated limb — simultaneously absurd and deeply human — has become the conversation piece of the day, and it has reminded audiences why Pooja Bhatt remains one of Indian cinema's more compelling personalities beyond her film work.
