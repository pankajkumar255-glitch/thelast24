Gul Panag Reacts to Her Film 'Satluj' Being Pulled from ZEE5: 'Don't Stop Telling Difficult Stories'

Key facts

  • Film 'Satluj' featuring Gul Panag removed from ZEE5
  • Panag publicly urged: 'Don't stop telling difficult stories'
  • Removal raises concerns about creative freedom on Indian OTT platforms

Actress Gul Panag has broken her silence over the removal of her film 'Satluj' from ZEE5, issuing a pointed statement asking that the industry not abandon the telling of difficult stories. Her reaction signals both personal disappointment and a broader concern about the creative environment on Indian streaming platforms.

'Satluj' was pulled from ZEE5, though the reasons for its removal have not been publicly detailed by the platform. The incident has quickly escalated into a flashpoint for debates around OTT content regulation, platform editorial decisions, and the space available for stories that challenge mainstream sensibilities. Panag's appeal — 'Don't stop telling difficult stories' — carries the weight of an artist aware that such removals send a chilling message far beyond any single film.

India's OTT sector has exploded in reach and influence over the past several years, but it has also come under increasing scrutiny from regulators, political groups, and platform executives themselves, who must balance creative ambition with commercial and political considerations. Films and series perceived as sensitive or controversial have found themselves navigating an increasingly complex landscape.

Panag's public stance joins a growing chorus of voices in Indian entertainment calling for greater transparency from platforms when content is restricted or removed. The episode raises a fundamental question that the industry will have to grapple with: in a media environment with unprecedented reach, who decides which difficult stories get told?
