Indian Companies Have Invested $40 Billion and Created 425,000 Jobs in the US

Key facts

  • Indian firms have invested $40 billion in the US
  • 425,000 jobs created in the US by Indian companies
  • Spans IT, pharma, manufacturing and other sectors
  • Data highlighted amid India-US trade negotiations

Indian companies have invested $40 billion in the United States and created 425,000 jobs there, figures that reframe the India-US economic relationship beyond the usual narrative of Indian IT workers filling American offices. The data, as explained by The Indian Express, highlights a two-way investment relationship that is far more substantial than is commonly appreciated by the general public on either side.

The scale of Indian corporate investment in the US spans sectors including information technology, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, and financial services. Major Indian conglomerates and mid-sized firms alike have built significant American operations — setting up factories, acquiring companies, and establishing research centres that employ hundreds of thousands of Americans directly.

The timing of this data being highlighted is notable. India and the United States are currently engaged in negotiations over trade terms, tariffs, and a broader bilateral trade agreement. The $40 billion investment figure and the 425,000 jobs created give Indian diplomats and trade negotiators a powerful counterpoint in discussions where the conversation often centres on the US trade deficit with India or the movement of American jobs.

For Indian readers, the numbers also illustrate how Indian multinationals — from Tata and Infosys to Sun Pharma and Wipro — have transformed into genuine global employers, not merely exporters. The story of Indian capital creating American jobs is one that is likely to feature prominently as both governments seek to deepen their strategic and economic partnership.
