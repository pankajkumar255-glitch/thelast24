Diljit Dosanjh and Director Honey Trehan Top IMDb's Popular Indian Celebs Chart After Film Takedown

Key facts

  • Honey Trehan and Diljit Dosanjh top IMDb's popular Indian celebrities list
  • Rise linked to public interest following Satluj's takedown
  • IMDb rankings driven by real-time user search activity

Honey Trehan and Diljit Dosanjh have claimed the top spots on IMDb's list of most popular Indian celebrities, a direct consequence of the controversy surrounding the takedown of their film Satluj. The removal of the film from circulation, far from diminishing its profile, has ignited widespread public interest and sent audiences searching for both the movie and its creators online.

IMDb's popularity rankings are driven by user search activity, making the duo's rise to the top a real-time measure of how deeply the Satluj controversy has resonated with Indian audiences. Honey Trehan, a respected director known for his work in the Mumbai noir space, finds himself in an unusual spotlight, while Diljit Dosanjh — already one of India's most bankable cross-cultural stars — has only added to his formidable public presence.

The takedown of Satluj has prompted significant debate around content regulation, freedom of expression, and the mechanisms by which films can be removed from public access in India. Each cycle of debate has fed further curiosity, creating a feedback loop that keeps both names at the top of the charts.

As reported by The Indian Express, the rankings reflect a broader pattern where controversy around Indian content — whether driven by censorship, legal challenges, or political pressure — tends to boost rather than suppress audience interest. For Trehan and Dosanjh, the IMDb milestone is an unexpected, if bittersweet, form of vindication.
