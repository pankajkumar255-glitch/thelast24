Cricket Legend Slams India Coach for Telling Vaibhav Sooryavanshi to 'Bide His Time'

Key facts

  • Cricket legend publicly criticises India head coach
  • Debate centres on Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's place in India team
  • Coach reportedly advised Sooryavanshi to 'bide his time'

A prominent cricket legend has publicly taken aim at India's head coach, criticising the suggestion that prodigious youngster Vaibhav Sooryavanshi should 'bide his time' rather than be given an extended and immediate run in the national setup. The sharp rebuke, reported by News18, signals growing unease among former players about how the team management is handling the teenage sensation.

Sooryavanshi burst onto the international scene and quickly captured the imagination of Indian cricket fans with his fearless batting approach. His emergence has raised difficult questions for the selectors and coaching staff about how to balance the claims of experienced players with the need to nurture and back a rare young talent.

The coaching staff's apparent position — that Sooryavanshi must wait and prove himself further before cementing his place — has drawn sharp pushback. The unnamed cricket legend's intervention adds significant weight to the argument that the youngster should be given a longer, uninterrupted run in the team rather than being managed cautiously from the sidelines.

The debate sits alongside a separate but related controversy over Sanju Samson's place in the team, with former India stars also speaking up in defence of Samson amid questions about whether Sooryavanshi should replace him. The dual debate underscores the difficult selection decisions confronting India's cricket establishment as it seeks to balance youth and experience.
