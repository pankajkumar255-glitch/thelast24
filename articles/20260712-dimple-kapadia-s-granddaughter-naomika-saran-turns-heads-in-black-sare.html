Dimple Kapadia's Granddaughter Naomika Saran Turns Heads in Black Saree at 'The Odyssey' Event

Key facts

  • Naomika Saran is Dimple Kapadia's granddaughter
  • Wore a black saree at 'The Odyssey' premiere event
  • Appearance described as bringing 'effortless elegance'

Naomika Saran, granddaughter of Bollywood legend Dimple Kapadia, made a graceful and much-discussed appearance at a premiere event for 'The Odyssey', arriving in a black saree that showcased what commentators described as effortless elegance. The outing adds to a growing series of public appearances that are steadily building her profile.

Naomika is the daughter of actress Simple Kapadia's family lineage — part of a Bollywood dynasty that includes her grandmother Dimple Kapadia, one of Hindi cinema's most enduring and celebrated actresses. Her willingness to attend high-profile film events in traditional Indian attire, chosen with evident care, has caught the attention of both fashion watchers and film industry observers.

Though Naomika has not yet made her formal Bollywood debut, her appearances at industry events are being treated as the groundwork for a possible entry into films. The film industry and its audiences have a long history of watching the next generation of star families with keen anticipation.

For now, she remains a compelling presence on the periphery of the industry, with her poise and aesthetics suggesting that when she does step in front of the camera, she will do so with considerable preparation.
