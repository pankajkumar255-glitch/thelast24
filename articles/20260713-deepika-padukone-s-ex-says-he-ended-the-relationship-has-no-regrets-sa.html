Deepika Padukone's ex says he ended the relationship, has no regrets: 'Said with respect'

Key facts

  • Deepika Padukone's former partner claimed he ended the relationship
  • He said the statement was made 'with respect' and has no regrets
  • Deepika Padukone is currently married to Ranveer Singh

A former partner of Deepika Padukone has doubled down on his claim that he was the one who ended their relationship, telling media that he has no regrets about saying so and that the statement was made with respect. The remarks, reported by The Indian Express, surface without specific context about which past relationship is being discussed, but are certain to stoke considerable interest given Deepika's status as one of India's most followed celebrities.

The phrasing 'said with respect' is notable — it suggests an awareness of the sensitivity of the claim and a deliberate attempt to position it as a statement of fact rather than a slight. That the individual chose to return to the topic and address it publicly indicates the matter has attracted renewed attention, possibly in the context of a larger interview or media appearance.

Deepika Padukone is married to actor Ranveer Singh and has spoken selectively in the past about her previous relationships. The reemergence of commentary about her romantic history from a former partner puts her at the centre of a conversation she has not directly participated in.

For audiences, the story is a reminder of the intense and often unwelcome scrutiny that female stars face regarding their personal histories — commentary that rarely flows in the other direction with the same frequency or intensity.
