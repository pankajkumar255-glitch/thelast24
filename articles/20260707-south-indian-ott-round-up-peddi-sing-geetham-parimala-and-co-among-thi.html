South Indian OTT round-up: Peddi, Sing Geetham, Parimala and Co. among this week's releases

Key facts

  • New South Indian OTT releases this week include Peddi, Sing Geetham, and Parimala and Co.
  • Releases span multiple South Indian languages and genres

This week's South Indian OTT slate brings a diverse set of releases for audiences streaming in Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam. Among the titles arriving on platforms are Peddi, Sing Geetham, and Parimala and Co., each catering to different audience tastes within the broad and varied South Indian market.

The consistent flow of South Indian content onto streaming platforms reflects a structural shift in how regional cinema reaches its audience. Where theatrical windows once defined a film's commercial fate, OTT platforms have become first-choice destinations for a significant portion of South Indian releases — particularly mid-budget and family-oriented titles that may not command the multiplex infrastructure of larger productions.

For viewers, the weekly OTT drop has become a reliable source of fresh content across genres — from drama and comedy to thriller and musical. The titles landing this week span enough variety to keep most tastes covered, whether audiences are looking for grounded family stories, lighthearted entertainment, or something more experimental.

The South Indian OTT market continues to grow rapidly, with platforms investing heavily in acquiring and commissioning regional-language content. This week's releases add to a catalogue that, in aggregate, now represents one of the most prolific regional content ecosystems on any streaming platform globally.
