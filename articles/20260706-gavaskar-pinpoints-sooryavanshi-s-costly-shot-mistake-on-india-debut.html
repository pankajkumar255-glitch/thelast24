Gavaskar Pinpoints Sooryavanshi's Costly Shot Mistake on India Debut

Key facts

  • Gavaskar identified a specific costly shot mistake by Sooryavanshi on debut
  • Sooryavanshi is one of India's most hyped teenage batting prospects
  • A former international predicted Sooryavanshi could play the 2027 World Cup

Sunil Gavaskar has offered a precise and candid assessment of Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's India debut, pinpointing a costly shot-selection error that the teenage prodigy made on his first appearance in international cricket. The legendary opener, whose own technique was considered near-perfect across a distinguished career, identified the specific mistake as a key factor behind Sooryavanshi's underwhelming start at the international level.

Sooryavanshi had arrived at the senior India setup as one of the most talked-about young batting talents in recent memory, having made waves in domestic and franchise cricket with a maturity well beyond his years. His debut, however, did not go as planned, and the scrutiny that inevitably follows highly touted youngsters into international cricket arrived quickly.

Gavaskar's critique is significant not simply because of who is delivering it, but because of its surgical nature — rather than a broad comment on the youngster's potential, the former captain has drilled down to a specific technical lapse. That kind of detailed attention from a batting great can be invaluable for a young player still developing his game at the top level.

Separately, former international cricketers have also weighed in on Sooryavanshi's future, with at least one ex-player predicting the teenager could feature in the 2027 ICC World Cup if he continues to develop. For now, addressing the shot mistake Gavaskar has flagged will be the most immediate priority for both the player and India's coaching staff.
