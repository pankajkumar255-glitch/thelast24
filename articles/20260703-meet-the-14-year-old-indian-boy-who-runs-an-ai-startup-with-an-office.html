Meet the 14-year-old Indian boy who runs an AI startup with an office in Burj Khalifa

Key facts

  • 14-year-old Indian boy founded and runs an AI startup
  • Startup has office space in the Burj Khalifa, Dubai
  • Story reported by NDTV

A 14-year-old Indian boy has grabbed attention by founding and running an artificial intelligence startup that operates out of an office in the Burj Khalifa, Dubai's landmark skyscraper and one of the most recognisable business addresses on the planet. The story, highlighted by NDTV, has gone viral as a remarkable example of teenage entrepreneurship at the intersection of AI ambition and global aspiration.

While details about the startup's specific product or revenue were not provided in the report, the mere fact of a school-age founder securing a foothold in the Burj Khalifa — home to some of the world's most prestigious corporate offices — signals both serious intent and, likely, significant backing or early traction. Dubai has become a favoured destination for Indian tech entrepreneurs, offering a business-friendly regulatory environment and easy access to Gulf capital.

The story resonates at a moment when India is heavily investing in AI literacy and youth entrepreneurship through government programmes and private initiatives. It also reflects a broader generational shift, as young Indians increasingly treat technology entrepreneurship as a viable and early career path, aided by the accessibility of AI tools that lower the barriers to building software products.

Whether or not the startup scales into a major company, the 14-year-old founder's story is already serving a larger cultural purpose — inspiring a conversation about what India's education system, startup ecosystem, and families can do to identify and support exceptionally driven young innovators before they have to look offshore to build their ambitions.
