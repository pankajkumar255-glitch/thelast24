Lock Upp: Shresta Iyer evicted as Ram Kapoor pleads to exit the show

Key facts

  • Shresta Iyer, sister of cricketer Shreyas Iyer, evicted from Lock Upp
  • Ram Kapoor begged producers to let him leave the show
  • Lock Upp is a captivity-themed celebrity reality show

Lock Upp's latest episode delivered high drama on two fronts: Shresta Iyer, the sister of Indian cricket star Shreyas Iyer, has been evicted from the show, while veteran actor Ram Kapoor broke down and begged to be allowed to leave. The twin developments have generated significant buzz online, reflecting the reality show's formula of combining celebrity names with high-tension confinement.

Shresta Iyer's presence on Lock Upp had drawn attention partly due to her famous sibling, and her eviction marks the end of a stint watched closely by cricket and entertainment fans alike. Ram Kapoor, a well-known face on Indian television known for shows like Bade Achhe Lagte Hain, pleading to exit the show adds an uncomfortable emotional dimension — raising questions about the participants' wellbeing within Lock Upp's demanding format.

Lock Upp, the captivity-based reality show, has consistently generated controversy since its inception, placing celebrities in jail-themed settings with limited resources and constant scrutiny. The show's producers have built its appeal around exactly these moments of emotional breakdown and dramatic elimination.

The episode has triggered debate among viewers and commentators about the emotional cost of such formats and whether the show adequately supports its contestants. Ram Kapoor's future on the show and the response from production were not confirmed at the time of reporting.
