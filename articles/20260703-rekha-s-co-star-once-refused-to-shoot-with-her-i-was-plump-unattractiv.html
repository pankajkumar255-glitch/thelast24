Rekha's co-star once refused to shoot with her: 'I was plump, unattractive'

Key facts

  • An unnamed Rekha co-star refused a joint photo shoot with her
  • Cited feeling 'plump' and 'unattractive' as the reason
  • Rekha is widely regarded as one of Bollywood's most iconic actresses

A co-star of Rekha — one of Hindi cinema's most celebrated and enduring icons — has come forward with a surprisingly candid confession: he once declined to participate in a photo shoot alongside her, citing his own physical insecurities. Describing himself as 'plump' and 'unattractive' at the time, the actor's admission reveals the kind of self-doubt that rarely surfaces from within the industry's carefully curated public image.

The disclosure is striking given Rekha's own journey — she too faced relentless scrutiny about her appearance early in her career before transforming into a figure synonymous with glamour and timeless beauty. That a male co-star would feel overshadowed by her presence to the point of refusing a joint shoot adds an unusual dimension to conversations about image and confidence in Bollywood.

The Indian Express, which reported the revelation, did not name the co-star in the headline, leaving the identity as the central point of intrigue for readers. The story has nonetheless sparked significant engagement, touching on body image, male vulnerability, and the often-unacknowledged pressures that actors face in a visually-driven industry.

For fans of Rekha and followers of Bollywood history, the anecdote adds another layer to the mythology surrounding one of Indian cinema's most compelling figures — a woman whose very presence, it seems, once made a colleague feel he didn't belong in the frame.
