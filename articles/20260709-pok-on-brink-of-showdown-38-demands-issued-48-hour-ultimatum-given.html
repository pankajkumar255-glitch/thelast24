PoK on Brink of Showdown: 38 Demands Issued, 48-Hour Ultimatum Given

Key facts

  • 38 demands issued to PoK authorities
  • 48-hour ultimatum given to respond
  • Grievances include economic, political and rights issues
  • PoK has seen repeated unrest in recent years

Pakistan-occupied Kashmir is teetering on the edge of another significant confrontation, with a group presenting 38 demands to authorities and issuing a 48-hour ultimatum to respond, according to NDTV. The move signals a sharp escalation in the region's already volatile political environment, where protests and standoffs have become increasingly frequent in recent years.

The demands, numbering 38 in total, reportedly span a range of grievances including economic conditions, electricity and flour subsidies, and the broader question of political rights for residents of the territory. PoK has seen major protest movements in recent years, with demonstrators accusing Islamabad of treating the region as a resource to be extracted rather than a people to be governed.

The 48-hour ultimatum puts Pakistani authorities under immediate pressure to respond. Previous standoffs in PoK have on occasion turned confrontational, with security forces deployed against protesters. Whether the authorities engage or attempt to suppress the movement will shape the trajectory of events in the coming days.

For New Delhi, developments in PoK are never merely a neighbour's internal matter. India claims the territory as an integral part of Jammu and Kashmir and watches any instability there with strategic interest. A weakened Pakistani grip on the region, or a population growing more resentful of Islamabad's authority, feeds into the long-running geopolitical contest over the Kashmir valley's ultimate status.
