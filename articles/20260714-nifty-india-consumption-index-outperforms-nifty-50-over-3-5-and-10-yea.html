Nifty India Consumption Index outperforms Nifty 50 over 3, 5 and 10 years — top 5 ETFs to watch

Key facts

  • Nifty India Consumption Index beat Nifty 50 over 3, 5 and 10 years
  • Top 5 ETFs tracking the consumption theme identified by Livemint
  • Theme covers FMCG, retail, autos and consumer durables

The Nifty India Consumption Index has delivered superior returns compared to the Nifty 50 over the three-year, five-year and ten-year periods, Livemint reported, underscoring the durable appeal of India's domestic consumption story as an investment theme.

The index captures companies whose fortunes are closely tied to Indian household spending — from fast-moving consumer goods and retail to automobiles and consumer electronics. Its long-run outperformance of the Nifty 50 reflects the structural tailwind of a growing middle class, rising discretionary incomes and expanding credit access that has powered consumer-facing businesses over the past decade.

Livemint's report highlighted the top five ETFs that track the Nifty India Consumption Index, though the specific fund names were not enumerated in the headline. For retail investors, ETFs offer a low-cost, diversified way to participate in this theme without having to pick individual stocks — a particularly relevant consideration given the volatility that can affect single-sector bets.

The data arrives at a time when investors are evaluating how India's domestic demand story holds up against global headwinds including a rising trade deficit, commodity price pressures and uncertainty around the interest rate cycle. The consumption index's consistent long-term track record provides one of the clearer data points for those assessing where patient capital might be best deployed.
