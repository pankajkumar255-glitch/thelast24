Trump Tariff Uncertainty Brings Enquiries but Few Orders for Indian Exporters

Key facts

  • Indian exporters seeing more US enquiries amid Trump tariff uncertainty
  • Enquiries not converting into firm purchase orders
  • US buyers exploring alternatives to Chinese suppliers
  • Policy unpredictability in Washington cited as key reason for hesitation

Indian exporters are finding themselves in a frustrating limbo: American buyers are calling and emailing in greater numbers than before, drawn by the disruption Donald Trump's tariffs have caused to established supply chains, but very few of those conversations are turning into confirmed orders. The Times of India reported that the uncertainty surrounding the exact shape and longevity of the US tariff regime is the central reason for the hesitation.

The underlying logic for American companies is straightforward — with steep tariffs threatening to raise the cost of Chinese goods, India looks attractive as an alternative sourcing destination. Indian manufacturers of textiles, auto components, electronics, and pharmaceuticals have all noted an uptick in inquiries. But buyers are reluctant to switch suppliers, sign new contracts, or invest in new sourcing relationships when the tariff landscape could change again with a presidential tweet or executive order.

For India, the moment represents both a significant opportunity and a test of its export infrastructure. Competing with China requires not just price competitiveness but also the ability to deliver at scale, on time, and with consistent quality — areas where Indian industry has historically had to work harder to convince global buyers.

Trade bodies and export councils are urging the government to use this window to push bilateral trade negotiations with Washington and to reduce domestic transaction costs that make Indian exports less competitive. Whether enquiries convert into orders will depend as much on policy certainty in the US as on how quickly Indian exporters can demonstrate they are a credible, scalable alternative.
