Arjun Kapoor pens emotional note as sister Anshula Kapoor marries Rohan Thakkar

Key facts

  • Anshula Kapoor married Rohan Thakkar
  • Arjun Kapoor wrote 'You'll always be mine' in an emotional tribute
  • Anshula shared video of Rohan's first reaction to her bridal look

Anshula Kapoor, sister of actor Arjun Kapoor, married Rohan Thakkar in a ceremony that sent both the Kapoor family and the wider Bollywood world into a celebratory mood. Arjun Kapoor marked the occasion with an emotional public note directed at his sister, writing 'You'll always be mine' — a message that resonated widely online and underlined the siblings' close bond.

Anshula, who is known for her entrepreneurial work beyond the film industry, also shared a video capturing Rohan Thakkar's first, unguarded reaction to seeing her in her bridal look. The clip was described as 'priceless' and spread rapidly across social media platforms, adding a warm, personal dimension to what is otherwise a very public family event.

The Kapoor family has long occupied a central place in Hindi film culture, and Anshula's wedding drew attention from across the industry. Arjun's note, in particular, was seen as a touching sibling tribute that highlighted the family's emotional closeness despite the pressures and public scrutiny that come with being part of Bollywood royalty.

The wedding images and videos are expected to continue circulating through the week, making this one of the most-followed celebrity social events in recent months.
