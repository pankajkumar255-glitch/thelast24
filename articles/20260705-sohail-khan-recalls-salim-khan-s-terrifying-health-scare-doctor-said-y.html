Sohail Khan recalls Salim Khan's terrifying health scare: 'Doctor said you might lose him'

Key facts

  • Salim Khan slipped into a 'semi-coma', per Sohail Khan's account
  • Doctor warned the family: 'You might lose him'
  • Salim Khan is legendary screenwriter known for Sholay and Deewar

Sohail Khan has spoken with rare candour about a harrowing medical emergency involving his father, the iconic screenwriter Salim Khan, revealing that the veteran had slipped into a 'semi-coma' and that doctors had warned the family they might lose him. The disclosure, made in an interview carried by The Indian Express, offers an unusually intimate glimpse into one of Bollywood's most storied families during an extremely difficult period.

Salim Khan, who along with writing partner Javed Akhtar forged some of Hindi cinema's most enduring screenplays — including Sholay, Deewar, and Don — is a figure of enormous cultural significance. His health, therefore, commands attention well beyond his immediate circle. Sohail's account of the doctor's grim prognosis underlines how serious the episode was for the entire family.

The Khan family, which includes actors Salman, Arbaaz and Sohail, is among the most prominent in Indian entertainment. Sohail's decision to speak publicly about such a private family crisis reflects a broader shift among Bollywood personalities towards greater openness about health and vulnerability — a theme that has resonated strongly with Indian audiences in recent years.

Salim Khan has not made a formal public statement on his health at the time of this report. The family's willingness to discuss the incident, however, has drawn widespread empathy and a flood of well-wishes from fans and colleagues across the industry.
