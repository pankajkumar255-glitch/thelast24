Dipika Kakar Opens Up on Painful Side Effects After Second Round of Immunotherapy

Key facts

  • Dipika Kakar undergoing immunotherapy for cancer
  • Second round of immunotherapy causing painful side effects
  • Actress says her body is 'adjusting' to treatment

Dipika Kakar, one of Indian television's most beloved actresses, has shared a vulnerable update on her ongoing battle with cancer, revealing that her second round of immunotherapy has left her dealing with significant and painful side effects. In her own words, 'My body is adjusting,' a phrase that captures both the physical ordeal and the quiet resilience she is bringing to her treatment.

Immunotherapy, a form of cancer treatment that harnesses the body's own immune system to fight the disease, is known to cause a range of side effects as the body responds to the therapy. Kakar's candour about her experience is notable at a time when public conversations around cancer treatment in India remain relatively limited, often shrouded in stigma or silence.

Since first disclosing her diagnosis, Kakar has maintained a steady connection with her fanbase, sharing updates that balance honesty with hope. Her approach has drawn widespread admiration and has sparked broader conversations about health, resilience, and the importance of open dialogue around serious illness among public figures.

The entertainment industry and her fanbase have continued to rally around her with messages of support. Her journey, now followed closely by millions, serves as a reminder of the deeply personal battles that unfold behind the camera.
