Air India Only Indian Airline in Global Top 10 for Punctuality in June

Key facts

  • Air India ranked in global top 10 for on-time performance in June
  • Only Indian airline to feature in the top 10
  • Operated under Tata Group since privatisation
  • No other Indian carrier in the top tier

Air India has become the only Indian airline to rank among the global top 10 for on-time performance in June, according to a report carried by Hindustan Times, marking a landmark achievement for a carrier that was once notorious for delays and operational dysfunction during its years under government ownership.

The recognition covers punctuality data for June and places Air India alongside the world's best-performing airlines — a list typically dominated by carriers from the Gulf, East Asia, and Europe. The feat is particularly noteworthy given the scale of the transformation Air India has been attempting since Tata Group completed its acquisition: new aircraft inductions, cabin refurbishments, technology upgrades, and a large-scale crew retraining programme have all been underway simultaneously.

Punctuality is one of the hardest metrics to improve at scale in aviation, dependent on everything from aircraft turnaround times and slot management to maintenance reliability and crew rostering. Breaking into the global top 10 suggests Air India's operational backbone is strengthening, even as the airline continues to take delivery of new wide-body and narrow-body jets to replace its ageing fleet.

No other Indian carrier featured in the top-10 list, underlining the gap between Air India's current trajectory and the rest of the domestic industry. For consumers, consistent punctuality improvements would represent one of the most tangible benefits of the airline's privatisation — a development that matters directly every time they book a flight.
