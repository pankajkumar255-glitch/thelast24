Google selects 20 Indian startups for its India Startup Accelerator 2026

Key facts

  • 20 startups selected for Google's India Startup Accelerator 2026
  • Programme offers Google Cloud credits, mentorship, and investor access
  • Cohort announced by Inc42

Google has selected 20 startups for its India Startup Accelerator 2026, the company announced on Thursday, opening the latest chapter of its effort to identify and support high-potential Indian technology ventures at an early stage of their development.

The India Startup Accelerator is one of Google's flagship programmes for emerging markets and is regarded as a significant stamp of validation in India's startup community. Selected companies typically receive access to Google Cloud credits, hands-on technical mentorship from Google engineers, product strategy guidance, and introductions to Google's investor network — advantages that can meaningfully accelerate a company's growth trajectory and fundraising prospects.

The 2026 cohort, as reported by Inc42, comprises 20 startups, though the full list of selected companies covers a range of sectors that reflect the current priorities of India's technology ecosystem. The programme has historically been competitive, with hundreds of applicants vying for a limited number of spots each cycle.

The announcement comes at a moment when India's startup environment is recalibrating after years of exuberant growth followed by a funding winter. Google's continued investment in an India-specific accelerator signals ongoing confidence in the depth of entrepreneurial talent in the country, even as startups navigate a more demanding environment for raising capital. For the 20 selected founders, the programme represents not just resources but association with one of the world's most recognised technology brands at a formative stage.
