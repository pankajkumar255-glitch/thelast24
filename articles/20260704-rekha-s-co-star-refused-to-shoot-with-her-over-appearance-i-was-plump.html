Rekha's co-star refused to shoot with her over appearance: 'I was plump, unattractive'

Key facts

  • Rekha's co-star admitted refusing a photo shoot citing own appearance insecurities
  • Co-star described feeling 'plump' and 'unattractive' at the time
  • Rekha famously underwent a dramatic image transformation in her early career
  • Rekha is known for iconic films including Umrao Jaan and Silsila

A candid revelation about Bollywood's past has surfaced, with one of Rekha's former co-stars admitting they once refused to participate in a photo shoot alongside the actress — not out of rivalry, but out of a deeply personal insecurity about their own appearance, describing themselves at the time as "plump" and "unattractive."

The disclosure offers a rare, unguarded glimpse into the body-image anxieties that pervaded the Hindi film industry during Rekha's formative years in cinema. Rekha, who debuted as a teenager and faced significant public commentary about her weight and looks before transforming into one of the industry's most glamorous icons, was herself no stranger to appearance-based criticism during that period.

The unnamed co-star's admission inverts the usual narrative: rather than dismissing Rekha, they were paralysed by their own self-perceived inadequacy beside her. It is a humanising account that speaks to the relentless physical scrutiny all actors — not just women — faced in that era, and the psychological toll it exacted.

Rekha remains one of Hindi cinema's most enduring and enigmatic figures, celebrated for her performances in films like Umrao Jaan and Silsila. Stories from her early career continue to attract significant interest, both for what they reveal about the actress herself and for what they expose about the industry's often harsh internal culture during the 1970s and 1980s.
