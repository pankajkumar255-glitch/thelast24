MLB partners with Suryakumar Yadav to grow baseball's fan base in India

Key facts

  • MLB partners with Suryakumar Yadav to expand in India
  • Deal aims to leverage Suryakumar's popularity to grow baseball's fan base
  • Part of a wider American sports leagues push into the Indian market

Major League Baseball has partnered with Indian cricket sensation Suryakumar Yadav in a bid to grow the sport's following in India, Reuters reported, in a striking cross-sport collaboration that underscores the global appeal of India's sporting audience.

Suryakumar, widely regarded as one of the most entertaining batters in T20 cricket, brings with him a vast and devoted following across India and beyond. MLB's decision to align with him rather than a traditional baseball figure reflects a deliberate strategy to find cultural entry points into a country where cricket is a near-religion.

The partnership is part of a broader push by American sports leagues, including the NBA and NFL, to make inroads in India ahead of what many sports executives believe will be an explosion in sports consumption over the next decade. India's young, digitally connected demographic represents an enormous untapped audience for baseball.

For Indian sports fans, the partnership raises the intriguing possibility of greater access to MLB content and grassroots baseball programmes in India. Whether Suryakumar's star power can genuinely move the needle for a sport with virtually no domestic infrastructure in India remains to be seen, but the deal marks a meaningful step in that direction.
