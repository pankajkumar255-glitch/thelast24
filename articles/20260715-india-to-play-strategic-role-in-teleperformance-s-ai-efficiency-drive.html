India to play strategic role in Teleperformance's AI efficiency drive, says Global CEO Jorge Amar

Key facts

  • Teleperformance Global CEO Jorge Amar named India as strategic hub for AI programme
  • TP is one of the world's largest BPO and customer experience companies
  • India positioned as driver of AI efficiency, not just cost arbitrage
  • Remarks come amid sector-wide concern over AI's impact on BPO jobs

India will occupy a strategic role in Teleperformance's global artificial intelligence efficiency programme, the company's Global CEO Jorge Amar has said, in remarks reported by Moneycontrol. The statement elevates India's position within TP's global structure from a traditional outsourcing base to a central node for AI-driven operational transformation — a meaningful distinction as the sector reconfigures around automation.

Teleperformance, known as TP, is among the world's largest customer experience and business process outsourcing companies, with a substantial India presence across multiple cities. Amar's comments suggest the company intends to leverage India's engineering talent and operational scale to design, deploy, and refine AI tools that will improve efficiency across its global operations.

The framing is significant in the context of broader industry anxiety about AI's impact on BPO employment. Rather than positioning AI as a replacement for India operations, TP's global leadership appears to be casting India as a participant in building and running the AI systems — a higher-value role that would theoretically preserve and potentially upgrade the nature of employment in the sector.

For India's BPO industry, which employs hundreds of thousands across tier-1 and tier-2 cities, this kind of strategic repositioning by a global industry leader carries weight. It suggests a possible path where Indian operations evolve alongside AI rather than being displaced by it — though the scale and pace of such transitions will determine whether the net employment effect is ultimately positive.
