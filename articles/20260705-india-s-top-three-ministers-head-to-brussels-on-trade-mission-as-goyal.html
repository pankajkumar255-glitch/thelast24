India's Top Three Ministers Head to Brussels on Trade Mission as Goyal Updates on Canada, Israel and Peru

Key facts

  • Three senior Indian ministers travel to Brussels on trade mission
  • Goyal updates on Canada, Israel and Peru trade talks
  • India-EU free trade talks have been ongoing for over a decade
  • Multiple trade negotiations running simultaneously

Three senior Indian ministers have departed for Brussels on a dedicated trade mission, reflecting the accelerating pace of India's effort to clinch bilateral and regional trade agreements as a new global tariff landscape reshapes the incentives for deal-making, The Economic Times reported.

Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal also provided updates on negotiations with Canada, Israel and Peru, signalling that India's trade diplomacy is running on multiple parallel tracks. The India-Canada trade relationship has been complicated by diplomatic tensions in recent years, making any update on that front particularly noteworthy. Progress with Israel would carry strategic as well as economic dimensions, while a deal with Peru would expand India's footprint in Latin America.

The Brussels mission focuses attention on the long-running India-European Union Free Trade Agreement talks, which have experienced numerous delays and restarts since negotiations were first launched over a decade ago. Both sides have signalled renewed political will to conclude an agreement, driven in part by shared concerns about supply chain diversification and the need to reduce dependence on single dominant trading partners.

For Indian businesses — particularly in sectors like pharmaceuticals, textiles, IT services and automobiles — an EU deal would be transformative, opening one of the world's largest and wealthiest consumer markets on more favourable terms. The government's decision to send a high-level ministerial delegation underscores how seriously it is treating this window of opportunity.
