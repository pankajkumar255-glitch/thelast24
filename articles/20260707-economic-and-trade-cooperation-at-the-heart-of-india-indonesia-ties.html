Economic and Trade Cooperation at the Heart of India-Indonesia Ties

Key facts

  • Economic and trade cooperation confirmed as key pillar of India-Indonesia ties
  • Modi's Jakarta visit aimed at deepening bilateral economic engagement
  • India and Indonesia among Asia's largest and fastest-growing economies
  • Bilateral trade seen as below its potential with significant room to grow

Economic and trade cooperation remains a defining pillar of the India-Indonesia relationship, with both governments reaffirming their commitment to deepening bilateral engagement during Prime Minister Modi's visit to Jakarta, Business Standard reported.

India and Indonesia are two of Asia's largest and fastest-growing economies, and the complementary nature of their economic profiles makes the partnership particularly attractive. India brings strengths in information technology, pharmaceuticals, and services; Indonesia offers vast natural resources, a large consumer market, and a strategic location at the heart of Southeast Asia's maritime trade routes.

Modi's visit has provided a high-level political impetus to translate strategic intent into concrete trade and investment frameworks. Bilateral trade between the two countries has grown in recent years but remains below its potential, and both sides see room to significantly expand commercial ties across sectors including energy, digital infrastructure, and manufacturing.

Against the backdrop of shifting global supply chains and the broader India-led effort to build partnerships across the Indo-Pacific, the Indonesia visit reinforces New Delhi's strategic pivot toward Southeast Asia — a region it views as critical to its own economic and geopolitical ambitions in the coming decades.
