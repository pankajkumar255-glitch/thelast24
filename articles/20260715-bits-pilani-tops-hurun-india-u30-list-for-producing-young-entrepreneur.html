BITS Pilani tops Hurun India U30 list for producing young entrepreneurs, IIT Delhi second

Key facts

  • BITS Pilani tops Hurun India U30 entrepreneurship list
  • IIT Delhi ranked second on the list
  • Rankings based on highest number of young entrepreneurs produced
  • Reflects growing startup culture at India's engineering institutions

BITS Pilani has claimed the top spot on the Hurun India Under-30 list, edging out IIT Delhi to be recognised as the institution producing the greatest number of young entrepreneurs in the country. The ranking, reported by the Times of India, cements BITS Pilani's reputation as a breeding ground for startup founders and underscores a broader shift in how India's elite engineering colleges are being evaluated.

The Hurun India U30 list tracks young entrepreneurs under the age of 30 who have built notable businesses, and the institutional breakdown reveals where India's next generation of startup founders is being shaped. BITS Pilani's culture of academic flexibility, its strong alumni network, and its practice-school model — which places students in industry from early in their degree — are widely credited with fostering entrepreneurial thinking.

IIT Delhi's second-place finish reinforces the dominance of engineering institutions in India's startup ecosystem, even as business schools and liberal arts colleges push to close the gap. Together, the top two institutions reflect how deeply the startup aspiration has penetrated India's most competitive campuses.

The result carries real-world significance beyond college rankings. Venture capitalists and accelerators have long observed that founder pedigree matters in early fundraising conversations, and an institution's placement on lists like Hurun's can shape investment flows. For BITS Pilani, the top ranking is both a validation and a recruitment advantage as it competes with IITs for India's brightest students.
