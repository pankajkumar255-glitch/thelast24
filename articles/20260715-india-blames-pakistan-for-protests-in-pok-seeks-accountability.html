India Blames Pakistan for Protests in PoK, Seeks Accountability

Key facts

  • India formally blamed Pakistan for protests in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir
  • India sought accountability from Islamabad
  • The statement marks an escalation in India's diplomatic posture on PoK
  • PoK protests have historically been linked to economic grievances

India has directly blamed Pakistan for orchestrating protests in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir and demanded accountability from Islamabad, in what represents a notably assertive diplomatic statement on a long-contested issue. The charge, reported by The Hindu, accuses Pakistan of using the unrest in PoK as an instrument of state policy rather than a spontaneous expression of public grievance.

Protest movements in PoK have a complex history rooted in economic grievances, demands for cheaper electricity and flour, and resentment over Islamabad's administrative control. India has consistently argued that Pakistan suppresses genuine political rights in the territory while simultaneously exploiting local discontent to deflect international attention from its own governance failures.

By going public with the blame and explicitly seeking accountability, New Delhi is raising the stakes in a bilateral relationship already strained by the aftermath of recent military tensions. The move could be intended to draw international attention to conditions in PoK, countering Pakistani narratives about Indian-administered Jammu and Kashmir.

The statement is unlikely to yield an immediate diplomatic resolution but signals that India intends to contest the narrative around PoK more forcefully in international forums. The situation in the region continues to be closely watched by both governments and regional analysts, with any escalation in protests carrying the potential to complicate an already volatile bilateral dynamic.
