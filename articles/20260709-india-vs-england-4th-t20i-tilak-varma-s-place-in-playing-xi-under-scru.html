India vs England 4th T20I: Tilak Varma's place in playing XI under scrutiny

Key facts

  • 4th T20I of India vs England series
  • Tilak Varma's place in the playing XI in question
  • Series ongoing between India and England

India's team management faced a pointed selection question ahead of the 4th T20I against England: would Tilak Varma keep his spot in the playing eleven? The young batter has been part of India's T20 plans, but with a series to manage and combinations to fine-tune, his position was not guaranteed going into the fixture.

The India vs England T20I series has been closely contested, making every selection call consequential. The fourth match of the series offered both sides an opportunity to either extend their lead or level the contest, adding extra pressure to every team combination decision made in the dressing room.

Tilak Varma has shown flashes of his talent in the T20 format for India, but like many young players in a competitive squad, he has had to prove his consistency to hold down a regular berth. The question of whether he retains his XI spot reflects the broader challenge facing India's middle-order batters vying for a limited number of positions.

For Indian cricket fans, the 4th T20I provided another opportunity to watch a high-profile bilateral series against one of the sport's great rivals, with the match available on TV and online streaming platforms.
