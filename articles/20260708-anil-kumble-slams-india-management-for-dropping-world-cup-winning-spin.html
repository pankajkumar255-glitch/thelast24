Anil Kumble slams India management for dropping World Cup-winning spinner

Key facts

  • Anil Kumble publicly slammed India's team management over spinner exclusion
  • Kumble said the World Cup-winning spinner 'needs to play'
  • Criticism comes amid India's record T20I loss to England
  • Kumble is a former India captain and ex-head coach

Anil Kumble, one of India's greatest ever bowlers and a former captain, has delivered a withering verdict on India's team management, publicly demanding that a World Cup-winning spinner be included in the playing XI during the ongoing T20I tour of the United Kingdom.

'He needs to play,' Kumble said pointedly, making clear his frustration with selection decisions that have sidelined a bowler with proven credentials at the highest level. While the headlines do not name the spinner in question, the context of India's poor performance against England and the absence of an effective spin option makes the omission all the more glaring.

Kumble's intervention carries particular weight given his dual stature — as a record-breaking former India spinner who understands the demands of the position, and as a former head coach of the national team who has navigated selection politics firsthand. His willingness to speak out publicly, rather than through backchannels, signals that frustration among the cricketing establishment has reached a tipping point.

The criticism comes as India reel from a record T20I loss to England, with captain Shreyas Iyer himself calling the performances 'atrocious'. Whether the selectors respond to Kumble's challenge ahead of the next match in the series remains to be seen, but the pressure on the team management is now both internal and external.
