Tammy Beaumont to retire from international cricket after Lord's Test against India, ending a 17-year career

Key facts

  • Tammy Beaumont retiring after Lord's Test vs India
  • 17-year international career coming to an end
  • Lord's Test is her final international appearance
  • She is regarded as one of England Women's greatest batters

Tammy Beaumont, one of England Women's most celebrated batters, will retire from international cricket after the one-off Test against India at Lord's, ending a career spanning 17 years, The Times of India and India Today reported.

Beaumont has been a cornerstone of England's batting line-up across all three formats, accumulating runs at the highest level over nearly two decades of international service. Her decision to bow out at Lord's — cricket's most hallowed ground — gives her farewell a storybook quality that few players are afforded.

The announcement lends the India-England Women's Test an added layer of significance. Beyond the competitive stakes of the match itself, the game will now serve as a tribute to one of the sport's iconic figures, with fans and teammates alike knowing they are witnessing her final international innings.

For the Indian team, the occasion presents a unique dual challenge: winning on the field while also paying witness to a historic moment in women's cricket. Beaumont's retirement is part of a broader generational shift in the England Women's set-up, as the team looks to build towards future ICC events with a new core of players.
