World Cup Winner Exposes Team India's 'Major Flaw', Tells Agarkar to Pick Key Star

Key facts

  • A World Cup winner publicly identified a major flaw in Team India's setup
  • Chief selector Ajit Agarkar urged to pick a specific unnamed star player
  • Reported by NDTV Sports amid ongoing India squad selection debate

A former World Cup-winning cricketer has taken the unusual step of publicly identifying what they describe as a major flaw in the current India setup, and has directly called on chief selector Ajit Agarkar to select a specific player in response. The pointed critique, reported by NDTV Sports, intensifies the pressure on India's selectors at a time when squad composition debates are running particularly hot.

The nature of the 'flaw' and the identity of the recommended player were flagged as significant enough for a former world champion to go on record, a step that former internationals typically take only when they feel the issue is genuinely urgent. The framing of the criticism — that the flaw has been 'brutally exposed' — suggests a recent series or match performance brought the weakness into sharp focus.

Ajit Agarkar, who took over as India's chief selector, has faced ongoing scrutiny over the balance of squads across formats. Calls from respected former players carry weight in Indian cricket's ecosystem, where public opinion and expert criticism can influence selection conversations even if formal decisions rest with the board.

With India facing a packed schedule of international cricket, the selectors will need to weigh the merits of making changes now against the risks of disrupting a squad that has shown strength in other areas. The debate over this specific selection call is unlikely to end until either the change is made — or results on the field make the argument for them.
