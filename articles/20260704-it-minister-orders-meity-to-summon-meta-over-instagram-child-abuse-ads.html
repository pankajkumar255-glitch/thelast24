IT Minister Orders MeitY to Summon Meta Over Instagram Child Abuse Ads

Key facts

  • IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw orders MeitY to summon Meta
  • Issue: child abuse-related ads appearing on Instagram
  • Move marks a major escalation in India's big tech accountability push

IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has ordered the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology to formally summon Meta, the parent company of Instagram, following reports that advertisements related to child abuse appeared on the platform. The directive represents one of the sharpest government actions yet against a global technology company operating in India.

The development follows growing scrutiny of Instagram's content moderation practices in India. The surfacing of child abuse-linked advertisements on the platform has triggered alarm, with the minister's intervention suggesting the government views this as a red line that demands immediate corporate accountability. MeitY's summons will require Meta to explain how such content was allowed to appear and what safeguards are in place.

India is one of Meta's largest markets, giving New Delhi considerable leverage in regulatory dealings with the company. The government has previously pushed back against big tech platforms on issues of misinformation, data privacy, and compliance with local laws — but a summons directly tied to child safety marks a new and more urgent dimension to that relationship.

Meta will be expected to appear before MeitY officials and provide answers about its advertising algorithms and content review processes. The outcome could set a significant precedent for how social media platforms are held liable for harmful paid content that reaches Indian users, particularly minors.
