Harmanpreet Kaur calls Women's Test at Lord's 'a big moment for all of us'

Key facts

  • India Women to play a Test match at Lord's Cricket Ground
  • Captain Harmanpreet Kaur calls it 'a big moment for all of us'
  • Women's Test cricket at Lord's is a rare and prestigious occasion

India women's captain Harmanpreet Kaur has called the team's upcoming Women's Test match at Lord's Cricket Ground a historic occasion, saying it is 'a big moment for all of us'. Speaking to The Hindu, the captain's words carried a sense of occasion that goes well beyond the result of any single cricket match.

Women's Test cricket is played rarely compared to the men's game, which makes each such fixture — especially at a ground as storied and prestigious as Lord's — a genuine landmark in the calendar. For Indian women cricketers, many of whom grew up dreaming of playing at the sport's most revered venue, the moment carries a weight that Harmanpreet acknowledged openly and without reservation.

The Indian women's team has been on an upward trajectory in recent years, with growing crowds, broadcasting interest, and commercial investment transforming the landscape of women's cricket in India. A Test match at Lord's, the spiritual home of cricket, is both a reward for that progress and a platform to showcase the talent that has been built across the country.

Harmanpreet's comment — simple, direct and heartfelt — is likely to resonate across Indian cricket. For fans, selectors, and the next generation of players watching from home, the image of India's women taking the field at Lord's carries a significance that extends far beyond the cricket itself.
