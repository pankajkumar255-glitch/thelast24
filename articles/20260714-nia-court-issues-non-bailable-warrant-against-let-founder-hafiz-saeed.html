NIA court issues non-bailable warrant against LeT founder Hafiz Saeed in Pahalgam attack case

Key facts

  • NIA court issues non-bailable warrant against Hafiz Saeed
  • Warrant linked to the Pahalgam terror attack
  • Saeed is founder of Lashkar-e-Taiba, based in Pakistan
  • Saeed previously accused in 26/11 Mumbai attacks case

An NIA special court issued a non-bailable warrant against Lashkar-e-Taiba founder Hafiz Saeed on Monday in connection with the Pahalgam terror attack, formally naming one of the world's most wanted terrorists as an accused in the case that shook India earlier this year.

Saeed, who operates out of Pakistan and has long been designated a global terrorist by the United Nations and the United States, has been linked by Indian investigative agencies to the Pahalgam strike. The NIA's move follows a pattern of building a comprehensive legal dossier against Saeed, who was similarly named in the 26/11 Mumbai attacks case — highlighting a career of alleged terrorist orchestration spanning nearly two decades.

The issuance of a non-bailable warrant, while largely symbolic in immediate enforcement terms given Saeed's location in Pakistan, carries significant legal and diplomatic weight. It allows India to push for international cooperation, red-corner notices, and to formally present the case at multilateral forums including the Financial Action Task Force, where Pakistan's compliance with terror financing norms is under scrutiny.

The development comes amid sustained India-Pakistan tensions following the Pahalgam attack and subsequent military exchanges. Legal experts say the warrant also creates a formal record that can be used in India's diplomatic outreach to pressure Pakistan into action against groups operating on its soil.
