Dharma Productions locks Rs 250 crore deal to distribute Ramayana across India

Key facts

  • Dharma Productions signs Rs 250 crore India distribution deal for Ramayana
  • Deal is among the largest distribution agreements in recent Bollywood history
  • Karan Johar's Dharma Productions is the distributor

Dharma Productions, the production house founded by Karan Johar, has secured a Rs 250 crore deal to distribute the upcoming film Ramayana across India, marking one of the most significant distribution agreements the Hindi film industry has seen in recent years. The deal signals the sheer commercial weight being placed behind the mythological epic, which has long been anticipated as a major event film.

The scale of the distribution commitment — Rs 250 crore — reflects industry confidence that Ramayana will draw massive footfalls, but it also places enormous pressure on the film to recover costs at the box office. Distribution deals of this magnitude are typically reserved for films expected to cross blockbuster thresholds, and this one effectively sets a very high bar before a single ticket is sold.

For Dharma Productions, the deal represents a strategic pivot into big-tent event cinema at a time when the studio has been seeking to diversify beyond its traditional romantic-drama fare. Locking in distribution rights at this scale also gives Dharma significant leverage over how and where the film rolls out across the country.

The Ramayana film has been one of Indian cinema's most talked-about projects, with the source material carrying enormous cultural and religious resonance for audiences across the subcontinent. Whether Dharma's Rs 250 crore bet pays off will depend heavily on execution, casting reception and the competitive release landscape when the film eventually arrives.
