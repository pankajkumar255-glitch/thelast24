Karan Johar defends Alia Bhatt, calls her stardom 'undebatable' amid Alpha backlash

Key facts

  • Karan Johar publicly defended Alia Bhatt against Alpha critics
  • Called Bhatt's stardom 'undebatable'
  • Alpha has received mixed critical responses
  • Johar is a key figure behind the film's production

Filmmaker and producer Karan Johar has publicly slammed critics of Alpha, coming out strongly in defence of Alia Bhatt and asserting that the actress's stardom is 'undebatable'. His comments, reported by The Indian Express, are the most prominent industry pushback yet against what appears to be a divided critical and audience reception for the film.

Alpha has placed Alia Bhatt at the centre of what is being positioned as a major action franchise, and the stakes around its performance are considerable. Mixed reviews have prompted debate about whether Bhatt can carry a large-scale action film, a conversation Johar has now waded into with characteristic bluntness.

Johar's defence is notable not just for its content but for its timing. Coming amid active reviews and audience discourse, his public stance is a clear attempt to shape the narrative around Alpha and shore up confidence in its lead. In the competitive world of Bollywood, where opening-week perception can make or break a film's run, such interventions from heavyweight producers carry real weight.

The episode also underlines the ongoing tension between critical reception and star power in Hindi cinema. Whether Johar's vocal backing translates into renewed audience interest at the box office remains to be seen, but it has certainly ensured that Alpha and Alia Bhatt remain at the centre of the entertainment conversation this week.
