IFFM 2026 nominations out: Aamir Khan, Mammootty and Rishab Shetty among top contenders

Key facts

  • IFFM 2026 nominations announced
  • Aamir Khan, Mammootty, Rishab Shetty are top contenders
  • Festival is held annually in Melbourne, Australia

The Indian Film Festival of Melbourne has unveiled its 2026 nominations, spotlighting some of the biggest names in Indian cinema across languages and industries. Aamir Khan, Mammootty, and Rishab Shetty are among the high-profile contenders vying for top honours at the prestigious international festival.

The inclusion of Malayalam superstar Mammootty and Kannada icon Rishab Shetty alongside Bollywood's Aamir Khan reflects IFFM's increasingly pan-India focus — a recognition that Indian cinema's centre of gravity is no longer solely in Mumbai. Regional films and stars have been steadily gaining ground on the global festival circuit over the past few years.

IFFM, held annually in Melbourne, is one of Australia's largest film festivals and a significant platform for South Asian storytelling. For the films and performers nominated, the recognition carries both critical prestige and international marketing value.

The full awards ceremony will give audiences and industry watchers a clearer picture of which direction Indian cinema's global reputation is heading in 2026.
