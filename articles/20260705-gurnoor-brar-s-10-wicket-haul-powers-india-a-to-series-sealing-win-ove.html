Gurnoor Brar's 10-wicket haul powers India A to series-sealing win over Sri Lanka A

Key facts

  • Gurnoor Brar took a 10-wicket match haul against Sri Lanka A
  • India A sealed the series win against Sri Lanka A
  • Performance has triggered calls for Brar's Test debut
  • Brar is a left-arm spinner competing for a senior India spot

Gurnoor Brar announced himself as a genuine Test contender with a match-haul of 10 wickets as India A sealed the series against Sri Lanka A in commanding style. The left-arm spinner dominated the Sri Lankan batting lineup across both innings, combining sharp turn with consistent line and length to produce one of the most complete bowling performances seen in India A cricket in recent memory.

The 10-wicket haul — a benchmark that separates genuine match-winners from good performers — has set social media and the cricketing punditry alight with calls for Brar to be handed a Test debut. India Today asked the question directly: should the selectors now give him his first senior cap? It is a question that will be difficult to deflect given the scale of his achievement.

India A's series win over Sri Lanka A also provides valuable competitive context. The shadow tour was designed precisely to assess players on the fringes of the Test setup, and Brar has done everything asked of him and more. His ability to take wickets in bunches against an international-calibre opponent is the most important indicator selectors look for at this level.

With India's Test schedule ahead and the selectors likely to convene soon, Brar's name will be firmly on the table. Whether they take the bold step of handing him a debut remains to be seen, but his case has arguably never been stronger.
