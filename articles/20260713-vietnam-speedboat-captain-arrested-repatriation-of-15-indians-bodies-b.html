Vietnam speedboat captain arrested; repatriation of 15 Indians' bodies begins

Key facts

  • Vietnam arrested the speedboat captain after the capsize
  • 15 Indian tourists were killed in the accident
  • Repatriation of mortal remains has begun
  • Victims' families include those from Vellore and Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu
  • Police called it an 'avoidable incident'

Vietnam has arrested the captain of the speedboat that capsized and killed 15 Indian tourists, with police describing the incident as an 'avoidable' tragedy that should not have happened. The arrest comes a day after the deadly capsize, as outrage over the disaster mounted both in India and internationally.

India's embassy in Vietnam swiftly moved to begin the repatriation of the mortal remains of all 15 victims. Survivors of the accident have also started making their way back home to India, according to reports from The New Indian Express and The Times of India.

The human cost of the tragedy is stark. Families in Vellore and Tiruvannamalai districts of Tamil Nadu are among the bereaved, with some households having lost their sole breadwinner in the accident. The scale of the grief has prompted local authorities and the Tamil Nadu government to take note.

The Indian government has expressed condolences and is coordinating with Vietnamese authorities to ensure the swift return of the victims' remains. The captain's detention signals that Vietnamese authorities intend to pursue accountability, though questions remain about the safety standards governing tourist boat operations in the region.
