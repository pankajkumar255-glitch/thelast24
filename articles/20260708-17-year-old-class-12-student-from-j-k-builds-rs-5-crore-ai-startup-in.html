17-year-old Class 12 student from J&K builds Rs 5 crore AI startup in six months

Key facts

  • Founder is 17 years old and a Class 12 student from J&K
  • AI startup valued at Rs 5 crore
  • Built in six months

A 17-year-old Class 12 student from Jammu & Kashmir has built an artificial intelligence startup valued at Rs 5 crore in just six months, becoming one of the most striking examples of India's youngest entrepreneurial talent making a mark in the country's booming AI space.

The student's journey — from school classroom to a multi-crore valuation — has captured national attention at a moment when India's government and investors are aggressively pushing AI adoption across sectors. The fact that the founder hails from J&K, a region not conventionally associated with startup culture, makes the story all the more remarkable.

India Today, which reported the story, highlighted the achievement as emblematic of a broader shift: AI tools are lowering the barrier to entry for entrepreneurs, enabling even those without access to traditional startup ecosystems in Bengaluru or Mumbai to build scalable ventures. The six-month timeline from idea to a Rs 5 crore startup suggests both the power of AI-driven product development and the appetite of early-stage investors to back young founders.

The story is likely to fuel fresh conversations about how India's education system and government schemes can do more to identify and support young innovators, particularly from Tier-2 cities, smaller towns, and Union Territories that have historically been left out of the startup boom.
