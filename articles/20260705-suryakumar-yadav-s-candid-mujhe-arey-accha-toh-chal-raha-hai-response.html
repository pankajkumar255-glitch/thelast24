Suryakumar Yadav's candid 'Mujhe? Arey accha toh chal raha hai' response to India question goes viral

Key facts

  • Suryakumar Yadav's casual reply to an India question went viral
  • SKY said 'Mujhe? Arey accha toh chal raha hai' when asked about his India future
  • Clip spread widely during the India vs England T20 series
  • Response praised for its candour and relaxed confidence

Suryakumar Yadav has never been one to overthink, and a viral video clip from the India vs England T20 series perfectly encapsulates why he has become one of Indian cricket's most beloved figures. When asked a probing question about his India situation, the T20 captain responded with characteristic ease: 'Mujhe? Arey accha toh chal raha hai' — a phrase that roughly translates to 'Me? Hey, things are going well' — delivered with the kind of breezy confidence that has defined his entire cricketing persona.

The clip, reported by The Times of India, spread rapidly across social media platforms, drawing an outpouring of affection from fans who see in Suryakumar a cricketer unburdened by external noise. In a sport where press conferences and public appearances often produce carefully managed responses, his unfiltered candour stands out sharply.

Suryakumar's place in Indian cricket — particularly in the T20 setup — has occasionally been the subject of debate, given the churn in selections and the presence of other talented batters competing for spots. But his own apparent equanimity about his standing suggests a player at peace with his game and his place in the team.

For fans, the moment is more than just a funny clip — it is a reflection of the mindset of a batter who has consistently performed under pressure at the highest level. As India look to regroup after their loss to England, his attitude may well be the steadying influence the dressing room needs.
