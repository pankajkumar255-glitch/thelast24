Diljit Dosanjh's 'Satluj' pulled from ZEE5 in India just two days after release

Key facts

  • 'Satluj' removed from ZEE5 India just two days after release
  • ZEE5 confirmed the film 'will be unavailable in India'
  • No official reason disclosed by ZEE5 or producers
  • Diljit Dosanjh is the film's lead star

Diljit Dosanjh's latest film 'Satluj' was pulled from ZEE5 in India just two days after its digital release, with the streaming platform confirming that the film would be unavailable to Indian audiences. The sudden removal has left fans and industry observers scrambling for answers, as no official explanation was offered either by ZEE5 or the film's producers at the time of the takedown.

The development is highly unusual for the Indian OTT landscape. Films are rarely withdrawn from platforms so soon after release, and such incidents typically point to unresolved legal disputes — over content rights, censorship objections, or court orders — or to a contractual disagreement between the production house and the streamer. Neither ZEE5 nor Dosanjh's team had made a public statement addressing the specific cause as of Wednesday evening.

Diljit Dosanjh remains one of the biggest pan-India entertainment figures of the moment, commanding massive followings both as a musician and an actor. The controversy around 'Satluj' will inevitably amplify interest in the film even as it remains inaccessible to domestic viewers, with many fans already exploring workarounds or awaiting a theatrical or alternative digital release.

The episode adds to a growing conversation about the fragility of OTT content agreements in India, where streaming rights disputes between studios, distributors, and platforms have become increasingly common. For subscribers who paid for ZEE5 partly to watch 'Satluj', the removal raises pointed questions about the reliability of streaming libraries.
