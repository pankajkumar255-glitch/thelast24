Top-10 firms add Rs 1 lakh crore in market cap; Airtel and Bajaj Finance lead gains

India's ten most valuable listed companies collectively added Rs 1 lakh crore in market capitalisation over the past week, with Bharti Airtel and Bajaj Finance emerging as the top gainers. The broad-based rally signals renewed confidence in blue-chip Indian equities. Both companies outperformed their peers, driving the bulk of the aggregate wealth creation among the top-tier index heavyweights.

→ When the top-10 firms add Rs 1 lakh crore in a week, it directly lifts mutual fund portfolios and retirement savings for millions of Indian retail investors holding index or large-cap funds.

21:28 IST

Ram Mandir trust reveals ₹3,264 crore spent; silver donations melted, ornaments on display

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust has disclosed that ₹3,264 crore has been spent on the Ram Mandir in Ayodhya, opening its financial accounts to public scrutiny for the first time. The trust also revealed that silver donated by devotees has been melted and repurposed, and put on display ornaments from the temple including a Ramcharitmanas valued at ₹5 crore. Separately, the trust has accepted the resignations of senior figures Champat Rai and Anil Mishra.

→ The trust's financial disclosure — and the high-profile departures of Champat Rai and Anil Mishra — bring fresh public scrutiny to the governance and spending of one of independent India's most prominent religious construction projects.

14:06 IST

Sensex Sinks 1,680 Points, Nifty Closes Below 23,900 as Trump Ends Trade Truce

Indian equity markets suffered a sharp selloff on July 8, with the Sensex plunging 1,680 points and the Nifty50 closing below the 23,900 mark. The crash was triggered after US President Donald Trump declared an end to the trade truce, rattling global investor sentiment. The fall wiped out significant market capitalisation in a single session, with broad-based losses across sectors. Analysts pointed to five key factors driving the market mayhem.

→ A 1,680-point Sensex crash directly erodes the value of mutual funds and retirement savings held by millions of Indian retail investors.

23:37 IST

TCS Q1 FY27: Net Profit Rises 4.61% to Rs 13,349 Crore; Wage Hikes Dent Margins by 170 Basis Points

Tata Consultancy Services opened FY27 with a net profit of Rs 13,349 crore in the June quarter, a rise of 4.61% year-on-year, even as wage hikes weighed on margins by 170 basis points. The banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) segment led growth, while a weak rupee provided a tailwind to revenue. TCS also announced it has won multiple AI transformation deals, signalling strong demand in that segment. Revenue beat analyst estimates, according to Reuters.

→ TCS is India's largest IT employer and a bellwether for the sector — its results set the tone for how Indian IT navigates global macro uncertainty, AI adoption, and wage pressures this fiscal year.

22:33 IST

DBS India FY26 Net Profit Jumps 49% to Rs 1,020 Crore

DBS Bank India reported a sharp 49% jump in net profit to Rs 1,020 crore for the full financial year FY26, underlining the strong growth trajectory of one of the largest foreign banks operating in the country. The Singaporean lender has been steadily expanding its India franchise, boosted by retail and corporate banking growth. The strong performance highlights India's growing attractiveness as a banking market for global institutions.

→ A near-50% profit surge by a major foreign bank signals that India's banking sector remains one of the most profitable growth markets globally — good news for financial sector employment and credit availability.

Godrej Industries, Paytm, Kalyan Jewellers Among Top Market Gainers on July 10

Thursday's market rally produced a clear set of top gainers on Indian exchanges, with Godrej Industries, Indian Bank, Kalyan Jewellers, Paytm, and CDSL among the stocks that stood out on July 10. The broader market surge — with the Sensex up 828 points — provided a favourable backdrop for gains across sectors. The mix of names across financial services, consumer goods, and fintech reflects the wide-based nature of the day's rally.
