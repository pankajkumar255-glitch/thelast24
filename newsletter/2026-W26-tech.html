India and Japan sign pacts on AI, metals and energy after Modi-Takaichi talks

India and Japan have signed a set of bilateral agreements covering artificial intelligence, critical metals and energy, following talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Japanese leader Sanae Takaichi. The pacts signal a significant deepening of the two nations' strategic technology partnership, extending cooperation beyond traditional defence and trade into the frontier domains of AI and the supply chains for materials essential to modern technology. The agreements come amid a global race to secure critical mineral supplies and AI capabilities.

→ India-Japan AI and critical metals deals could directly shape the pace at which Indian semiconductor, EV battery and deep-tech industries develop — industries central to India's ambitions as a future technology powerhouse.

Flipkart GOAT Sale 2026 Kicks Off July 4 With Deals on iPhone 17, Samsung Galaxy S25 and More

Flipkart has announced that its GOAT Sale 2026 will begin in India on July 4, offering discounts on a wide range of premium products including the iPhone 17, Samsung Galaxy S25, and HP laptops. The sale is positioned as a major mid-year shopping event, targeting consumers looking to upgrade smartphones, computers, and other electronics. Flipkart's GOAT — Greatest Of All Time — branding signals an attempt to rival the scale and excitement of its own Big Billion Days and Amazon's Prime Day sales. The announcement is likely to trigger competitive pricing moves from rival platforms.

→ If you have been waiting to buy an iPhone 17, Samsung Galaxy S25, or a new laptop, July 4 is now the date to watch for the best prices of the season.

13:21 IST

Government sends notice to Telegram, sets 15-day deadline to take immediate action

The Indian government has issued a formal notice to Telegram, giving the messaging platform a 15-day deadline to take immediate measures against content or activities flagged by authorities. The notice signals an escalation in India's regulatory pressure on encrypted messaging apps, which have increasingly been scrutinised for allegedly facilitating illegal activity, misinformation, and criminal coordination. Telegram now faces the prospect of compliance action or potential restrictions if it fails to respond within the stipulated window.

→ Tens of millions of Indians use Telegram daily; a government-mandated crackdown or potential blocking would directly affect news channels, community groups, and businesses that run on the platform.

21:02 IST

Thiruvananthapuram to get one of India's first university townships, says Rajeev Chandrasekhar

Former Union Minister of State for IT Rajeev Chandrasekhar has announced that Thiruvananthapuram will be home to one of India's first university townships, a large-scale integrated academic and research campus concept. The township model envisions bringing together universities, research institutions, startup incubators, and residential facilities under one planned zone, designed to foster innovation and talent development. The announcement positions Kerala's capital as a significant hub in India's emerging knowledge-economy infrastructure.

→ A university township in Thiruvananthapuram could reshape higher education and startup opportunities for students and entrepreneurs across Kerala and southern India.

21:14 IST

Piyush Goyal leads business delegation to Greece to boost trade and startup partnerships

Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has headed a business delegation to Greece, with the visit centred on deepening trade ties and forging startup and technology partnerships between the two countries. Greece, as an EU member state, presents a strategically valuable gateway for Indian businesses seeking closer commercial relationships with Europe. The delegation visit signals India's broader push to diversify its trade partnerships and integrate its startup ecosystem into international networks.

→ New trade and startup corridors with an EU country like Greece could open fresh markets for Indian technology firms and entrepreneurs seeking European footholds.

18:12 IST

JPMorgan warns GenAI and geopolitics will keep India's IT sector under pressure

Global investment bank JPMorgan has cautioned that the twin forces of generative artificial intelligence and geopolitical uncertainty will continue to weigh on growth prospects for India's information technology sector. The warning signals that even as Indian IT giants navigate a post-pandemic slowdown, structural disruptions from AI automation and shifting global trade dynamics add a new layer of complexity. The assessment carries weight given JPMorgan's standing as a top-tier analyst of technology markets worldwide.

→ With India's IT sector employing millions and driving a significant share of export earnings, sustained growth pressure affects jobs, salaries, and the wider economy.
