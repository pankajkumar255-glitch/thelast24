22:19 IST

India discusses agricultural collaboration with Taliban government of Afghanistan

India has entered into discussions with Afghanistan's Taliban government on agricultural collaboration, according to The Hindu, in a carefully calibrated outreach that reflects New Delhi's pragmatic approach to engaging with Kabul despite not formally recognising the Taliban administration. Agricultural cooperation — covering areas such as seeds, fertilisers, irrigation and food security — represents a relatively low-friction entry point for bilateral engagement. The move underscores India's long-term strategic interest in Afghanistan and its connectivity to Central Asia.

→ India engaging with the Taliban-run Afghan government on agriculture, however tentative, has real stakes — Afghanistan is a gateway to Central Asian markets, and past Indian investment in Afghan infrastructure means New Delhi can ill afford to be shut out of Kabul's development calculus.

21:37 IST

Four top-10 firms add Rs 93,000 crore in market cap despite weak market; HDFC Bank and Airtel lead gains

Four of India's ten most valuable companies added a combined Rs 93,000 crore to their market capitalisation in the past week, even as broader equity markets remained under pressure. HDFC Bank and Bharti Airtel led the charge among the gainers, reflecting investor confidence in financials and telecom even amid global uncertainty. The gains stand in contrast to the overall subdued mood on Dalal Street, highlighting a divergence between blue-chip heavyweights and the wider market.

→ When India's biggest companies gain Rs 93,000 crore in market cap even in a weak market, it signals that large-cap quality stocks are where institutional money is hiding — relevant to anyone with mutual funds or index-linked investments.

20:26 IST

Stock market this week: Q1 earnings, inflation data and Middle East tensions to keep D-Street volatile

Indian equity markets are braced for a volatile week ahead as three simultaneous forces converge on Dalal Street: the start of the Q1 earnings season that will test stretched valuations, incoming domestic inflation data that could influence the RBI's rate outlook, and escalating Middle East tensions that are pushing up global oil prices and risk aversion. Nifty is seen eyeing fresh support around the 23,400 level as traders position defensively. Analysts say the combination of domestic and global triggers makes this one of the more unpredictable weeks of the year.

→ Q1 corporate results starting this week will directly determine whether India's stock market valuations are justified — the outcomes affect everything from your equity mutual fund returns to market sentiment for the rest of the quarter.

17:44 IST

Dhamaal 4 Crosses ₹65 Crore in Opening Weekend, Outpaces Welcome to the Jungle

Dhamaal 4, the Ajay Devgn and Arshad Warsi comedy, has crossed ₹65 crore at the box office in its first three days, outperforming the opening weekend of Welcome to the Jungle. The film's strong commercial debut cements the franchise's draw for multiplex and single-screen audiences alike. By Day 4, the film was reported to be nearing ₹70 crore domestically with a global haul of ₹9 crore already added.

→ A blockbuster opening for a mid-budget comedy franchise is a healthy signal for the broader Hindi film industry and the multiplex chains that depend on reliable commercial hits.

22:49 IST

HCLTech Q1FY27: Net profit rises 20% to ₹4,624 crore; dividend announced; workforce shrinks

HCL Technologies posted a 20% growth in net profit to ₹4,624 crore in the first quarter of FY27, while also announcing a dividend for shareholders. The strong profit performance came even as the company reported a reduction of over 3,200 employees during the quarter, reversing headcount gains from the previous quarter. The results signal steady demand for HCLTech's services business even amid industry-wide caution on hiring.

→ HCLTech's 20% profit jump and a dividend payout matter directly to India's millions of retail shareholders and mutual fund investors with IT-sector exposure.

19:49 IST

Aditya Birla Group to acquire Shell's Sprng Energy for $1.8 billion

Aditya Birla Group has agreed to acquire Sprng Energy, Shell's India renewables arm, in a deal worth $1.8 billion (approximately ₹17,200 crore). The transaction marks one of the largest renewable energy acquisitions in India this year and signals the Aditya Birla Group's major push into clean energy. Sprng Energy is a significant player in India's utility-scale solar and wind segment.

→ A $1.8 billion bet by one of India's biggest conglomerates on homegrown renewables signals how fast corporate India is racing to own green energy assets.
