This week in National

The 6 stories that mattered, 28 Jun–05 Jul.

18:17 IST

Prime accused in Bengal BJP worker's murder held; arrest count reaches 11

The prime accused in the murder of a Bengal BJP worker has been arrested, taking the total number of arrests in the case to 11. The case has been politically charged given the victim's affiliation and the ongoing tensions between political factions in West Bengal. The arrest of the prime accused is seen as a significant development in the investigation, which has drawn national attention and triggered political controversy.

→ Political violence in Bengal has been a persistent flashpoint — the arrest of the prime suspect in this case signals both police progress and the continued pressure on law enforcement to deliver accountability in politically sensitive murders.

17:03 IST

12-year-old beheaded, skull dumped in sewer; three minors detained for murder

A 12-year-old child was beheaded in a shocking crime, with the skull subsequently dumped in a sewer. Police detained three minors in connection with the murder. The gruesome nature of the killing — carried out apparently by juveniles — has sent shockwaves through the community and raised serious questions about juvenile crime and child safety. Authorities are investigating the circumstances and motive behind the crime.

→ A murder of this brutality, allegedly committed by minors against another child, forces a disturbing national conversation about juvenile violence, safety and the gaps in India's child protection systems.

14:50 IST

Sonam Wangchuk begins hunger strike at Jantar Mantar, six students join

Climate activist and innovator Sonam Wangchuk launched a hunger strike at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi, with six students joining him in the protest. Wangchuk joined the Cockroach Janata Party demonstration, saying he was 'forced to sit here' as his demands remain unchanged. The protest at Jantar Mantar — India's traditional hub for demonstrations — signals a fresh escalation of his long-running campaign for Ladakh's constitutional safeguards and statehood.

→ Wangchuk's hunger strike at Jantar Mantar directly keeps the spotlight on Ladakh's unresolved demand for constitutional protections — a matter that affects the rights and livelihoods of thousands of people in the region.

14:35 IST

PM Modi addresses Seychelles Parliament, calls Indian Ocean an 'Ocean of Opportunity'

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the Parliament of Seychelles, declaring that India's vision is to make the Indian Ocean an 'Ocean of Opportunity'. He emphasised that the Indian Ocean 'connects us, not separates us', and highlighted the climate crisis facing small island nations. The visit, part of India's intensified Indian Ocean diplomacy, underscores New Delhi's strategic intent to deepen ties with island nations in its maritime neighbourhood.

→ Modi's Seychelles address is a signal of India's growing maritime diplomacy — as China expands influence in the Indian Ocean, New Delhi's outreach to island nations directly affects regional security and trade routes that matter to every Indian.

23:21 IST

Siya Goyal Taken to Lohagad Fort as Pune Police Recreate Ketan's Murder Scene

Pune police took Siya Goyal, accused of murdering her fiancé Ketan Chaudhary at Lohagad Fort, back to the crime scene to recreate the sequence of events. Investigators are probing reports that Goyal allegedly wanted "three years of freedom" and had planned the killing, with details emerging about her lover's prior visit to the fort as part of what police describe as a calculated strategy. The case has gripped national attention for its details of a relationship turned fatal. The crime-scene recreation is a standard investigative step to corroborate or challenge the accused's statement.

→ The Pune fort murder case has exposed how premeditated intimate-partner violence can unfold, raising uncomfortable questions about the pressures and dynamics in young relationships that Indian families and courts will have to grapple with.

21:09 IST

After Northern Wins, BJP Eyes South With Fresh Agenda for Telangana Polls

Fresh from back-to-back electoral victories in northern states, the BJP is now turning its attention to southern India, with Telangana identified as the next key target. New BJP national president Nitin Nabin articulated the party's pitch during a three-day visit, framing the campaign around development politics and contrasting it with what he described as dynasty politics and corruption. The Hindustan Times and The Hindu both reported on Nabin's messaging and the strategic significance of the visit. Telangana is currently governed by the Congress, which won the state in 2023.

→ BJP's push into Telangana signals a genuine contest for southern India's political future — a region where the party has historically struggled, and where success would dramatically reshape the national political map.
