Lion Snatches 11-Year-Old Boy from Hill in Gujarat, Mauls Him to Death in Forest

A lion snatched an 11-year-old boy from a hill in Gujarat and dragged him into a nearby forest, where the child was mauled to death. The incident highlights the recurring and deadly human-wildlife conflict in Gujarat, home to the world's only wild Asiatic lion population concentrated in and around the Gir forest region. Such attacks have been increasing as lion numbers grow and their range expands beyond protected areas into villages and agricultural land.

→ With Asiatic lions expanding their territory into human settlements in Gujarat, this tragedy is a stark reminder that human-wildlife conflict is a growing safety crisis for communities living at the forest fringe — with no easy policy solution in sight.

14:49 IST

Omar Abdullah Slams BJP Over J&K Statehood Delay, Alleges 'Backdoor' Political Manoeuvring

Jammu & Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah escalated his public confrontation with the BJP over the long-pending restoration of statehood to J&K, pointedly asking 'What will be the right time?' and adding, 'If not Delhi, should we go to the White House for statehood?' Abdullah also alleged that BJP was engaged in 'backdoor' politics in J&K, including claims of attempts to poach legislators using offers of money and ministry positions. The statehood question has remained unresolved since J&K was converted into a Union Territory in 2019.

→ Omar Abdullah's public ultimatum sharpens a constitutional standoff: J&K has been governed as a Union Territory for over five years, and the elected government's frustration over Delhi's continued silence on statehood directly affects the democratic and administrative rights of the region's people.

13:18 IST

Delhi Police Arrest 3 Alleged Lawrence Bishnoi Gang Shooters for Plotting Targeted Killing

Delhi Police have arrested three individuals identified as shooters linked to the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, who were allegedly plotting a targeted killing in the capital. The arrests come amid a sustained national crackdown on the Bishnoi gang's operations, which have been linked to high-profile killings and extortion rackets across India. The gang has been behind several brazen attacks in recent years, often using locally recruited shooters.

→ The Lawrence Bishnoi gang has become one of India's most feared organised crime networks, and arrests of its alleged operatives in Delhi signal that the capital itself is on the gang's target map — directly threatening public safety in the city.

13:12 IST

Sonam Wangchuk Urges Citizens to Join Protest as Health Worsens on Day 15 of Hunger Strike

Climate activist and innovator Sonam Wangchuk, on the 15th day of his hunger strike, urged Indian citizens to join his protest, saying 'I am not Gandhi, be your own hero.' Reports indicate Wangchuk's health has visibly deteriorated as his fast, organised under the Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) banner, enters its third week. Wangchuk has been demanding statehood for Ladakh and constitutional safeguards for the region's land, jobs, and environment.

→ Wangchuk's fast — now in its third week — keeps the unresolved question of Ladakh's constitutional status in national focus: residents of the Union Territory want the same protections other hill states enjoy, and the longer his health declines, the sharper the political pressure on Delhi becomes.

23:55 IST

NIA arrests two more absconders in Praveen Nettaru murder case

The National Investigation Agency arrested two more absconders in connection with the murder of Praveen Nettaru, the BJP Yuva Morcha leader who was killed in Karnataka's Dakshina Kannada district in 2022. The fresh arrests indicate the NIA is continuing to track down all accused in the high-profile case, which sparked widespread protests and political controversy at the time. The case has been under NIA investigation due to its suspected links with organised communal violence.

→ The Praveen Nettaru murder case has remained a politically charged touchpoint in Karnataka — each new NIA arrest keeps the case alive and intensifies pressure on the accused network.

22:05 IST

J&K Police arrest three publishers over books allegedly glorifying militants and separatism

Jammu & Kashmir Police arrested three publishers for allegedly producing and distributing books that glorify militants and promote separatism. The arrests mark a significant escalation in authorities' efforts to clamp down on what they describe as pro-insurgency literature circulating in the region. The action underlines the continuing sensitivity around free expression and national security narratives in J&K.

→ The arrest of publishers over book content in J&K raises pressing questions about the boundaries of free expression and the state's approach to counter-narratives in a region still navigating its post-Article 370 reality.
