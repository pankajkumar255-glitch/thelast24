The 6 stories that mattered, 28 Jun–05 Jul.

18:48 IST

China's New AI Cybersecurity Tool Said to Match Anthropic's Offering

China has developed a new AI-powered cybersecurity tool that researchers claim is on par with Anthropic's Mythos, a significant benchmark in the AI security space. The development signals China's accelerating push to close the gap with leading Western AI labs in specialised, high-stakes domains like cybersecurity. If the capability claims hold up to scrutiny, it would represent a meaningful advance in China's indigenous AI capabilities beyond general-purpose large language models.

→ An AI cybersecurity tool from China matching a leading American rival matters to every Indian business and government body that must navigate an increasingly AI-powered threat landscape — where the tools of attack and defence are advancing simultaneously.

14:32 IST

Google Caps Meta's Use of Gemini AI Amid Capacity Crunch

Google has placed limits on Meta's access to its Gemini AI models, citing a capacity shortfall, according to reports by the Financial Times and confirmed by multiple outlets including Bloomberg and NDTV Profit. The move has directly hit Meta's AI operations, which had been relying on Gemini as part of its AI infrastructure. The development highlights the growing strain on AI compute resources as demand from major technology companies surges far ahead of available supply.

→ When two of the world's biggest tech giants — Google and Meta — are battling over AI compute access, it signals how scarce and strategically critical AI infrastructure has become, with ripple effects for Indian startups and enterprises that depend on these platforms.

09:52 IST

OpenAI Launches GPT-5.6 Series: Meet Sol, Terra, and Luna

OpenAI has unveiled its GPT-5.6 model series, comprising three distinct AI models — Sol, Terra, and Luna. Sol, the first to launch, comes with a new reasoning mode and enhanced safety features. Despite the announcement, access remains extremely limited, with almost no users able to use the models yet. The release marks a significant step in OpenAI's push to build more specialised, tiered AI systems.

→ As AI becomes a bigger part of everyday work and creativity, knowing what the world's leading AI lab is building next — and when Indians can actually access it — is directly relevant to millions of students, developers, and professionals.

14:58 IST

OpenAI Unveils Sol, Terra and Luna AI Models — US Government Blocks Wide Release

OpenAI has announced three new AI models named Sol, Terra and Luna, but the US government has stepped in to block their wide public release. The move follows a pattern of Washington scrutinising powerful AI systems before permitting broad deployment. The intervention signals that at least some of OpenAI's latest models were deemed significant enough to warrant government oversight before reaching the general public. OpenAI had reportedly briefed US authorities on the models' capabilities ahead of the announcement.

→ OpenAI's models underpin AI tools used by millions of Indians — any US government restriction on their rollout directly shapes what AI capabilities are accessible in India and when.

09:44 IST

US Grants Limited Access to Anthropic's Mythos AI Model After National Security Review

The United States has approved limited access to Anthropic's Mythos AI model after previously blocking it over national security concerns. The rollout is restricted rather than open, reflecting the outcome of a security review process. The decision marks a partial green light for one of Anthropic's advanced AI systems, allowing controlled deployment rather than broad public release. It underscores Washington's growing practice of vetting powerful AI models before permitting their wider distribution.

→ As Indian AI researchers and companies increasingly look to access cutting-edge US AI models, US government gatekeeping of advanced AI directly affects what tools are available globally — and to Indian developers and enterprises.

17:07 IST

China Develops AI Cybersecurity Tool Said to Match Anthropic's Mythos

China has developed a new artificial intelligence-based cybersecurity tool that researchers describe as being on par with Anthropic's Mythos model in capability, according to NDTV. The development marks a significant milestone in China's effort to build frontier AI systems independent of Western technology, and in a domain — cybersecurity — that carries acute strategic implications. The tool's emergence adds a new dimension to the global AI competition, particularly as the US has been tightening export controls on advanced AI to China.

→ A Chinese AI cybersecurity tool matching Western frontier models raises real concerns about the global AI arms race and has direct implications for India's own digital security landscape.
