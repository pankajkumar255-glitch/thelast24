Ali Fazal Was Warned Against Signing Mirzapur: 'Filmmakers Asked What I Was Doing'

Ali Fazal has revealed that established filmmakers cautioned him against taking on his role in Mirzapur, the Amazon Prime Video crime drama that went on to become one of India's most popular web series. The actor recalled peers and industry figures questioning his decision to move into digital content at a time when OTT platforms were not yet seen as a credible career move for film actors. The disclosure offers a candid window into industry scepticism toward streaming before its explosive rise.

→ Fazal's account is a reminder of how completely OTT has upended Bollywood's hierarchy — and how actors who took early bets on digital platforms ended up shaping the era rather than being sidelined by it.

Laapataa Ladies Actor Satendra Soni Alleges Death Threat on Set; Film Body Demands FIR

Satendra Soni, known for his role in the acclaimed film Laapataa Ladies, has alleged that a filmmaker threatened to kill him on set. The allegation has triggered a swift response from a film industry body, which has backed Soni and is now seeking the registration of an FIR against the filmmaker. The incident has cast a spotlight on safety and power dynamics on Indian film sets.

→ The demand for a formal police complaint signals that Bollywood's industry bodies are under pressure to take on-set safety and conduct disputes seriously — something that directly affects thousands of daily-wage workers and junior artists who have little recourse.

Shah Rukh Khan's Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa Co-Star Refused to Play His Sister Because She Was in Love With Him

An actress who starred alongside Shah Rukh Khan in the 1994 cult classic Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa has revealed that she turned down the role of his sister in the film because she was in love with the actor. The candid disclosure sheds new light on the making of one of SRK's most beloved early films, which is widely regarded as a defining performance in his career. The revelation has resurfaced interest in the film and its behind-the-scenes dynamics.

→ The anecdote is a charming piece of Bollywood history — and a reminder of how Shah Rukh Khan's early career inspired the kind of devotion that shaped the casting decisions of films that fans still cherish decades later.

AI in Entertainment: Copyright Headaches Loom as Studios Embrace Generative Tools

India's entertainment companies are increasingly turning to artificial intelligence to generate content, but the adoption is bringing complex copyright questions in its wake. A single AI prompt can draw on vast amounts of existing creative work — scripts, music, imagery — raising unresolved questions about ownership, licensing, and liability. As Indian studios and streaming platforms explore AI-assisted production, legal frameworks have not kept pace with the technology.

→ For India's massive creative workforce — writers, composers, lyricists and designers — the unresolved copyright questions around AI-generated content could directly threaten livelihoods and the value of their original work.
