The 6 stories that mattered, 05 Jul–12 Jul.

17:14 IST

Newlywed woman dies after fall from Delhi terrace just two months after wedding; family alleges murder

A woman died after falling from a terrace in Delhi, only two months after her wedding, with her family alleging she was murdered. The case has drawn significant attention given the circumstances — a newly married woman dead under suspicious conditions — and police are investigating the incident. Her family has refused to accept the death as accidental and has demanded a thorough probe.

→ Suspicious deaths of young brides in India — especially so soon after marriage — often signal domestic violence or dowry-related crimes, making this a deeply important story for women's safety advocates and policymakers alike.

14:52 IST

Prashant Kishor to make electoral debut as Jan Suraaj fields him for Bankipur by-election

Political strategist-turned-activist Prashant Kishor will contest the Bankipur assembly by-election in Bihar on a Jan Suraaj Party ticket, marking his first foray into electoral politics as a candidate. Kishor, who built his reputation advising parties across the spectrum — from the BJP to the Congress to the TMC — founded Jan Suraaj as a grassroots movement to reform Bihar's governance. The Bankipur seat, located in Patna, will be a high-profile test for his political credibility.

→ Whether the man who scripted victories for others can win his own seat will determine whether Jan Suraaj becomes a credible political force in Bihar — or remains a movement without electoral legs.

14:10 IST

India hits back at Pakistan's Khawaja Asif, calls him 'mentally unstable' over remarks on PM Modi

Indian government sources have sharply rebuked Pakistan Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, calling him 'mentally unstable' after he made remarks targeting Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The response reflects the ongoing diplomatic hostility between the two countries, with New Delhi showing no hesitation in responding forcefully to provocations from Islamabad. The exchange marks another escalation in public rhetoric between the neighbours.

→ The public war of words between India and Pakistan at ministerial level keeps bilateral tensions firmly in view for Indian citizens tracking the fraught relationship with Islamabad.

14:28 IST

Wrong-side driving has killed 460 people on Maharashtra highways in 2025 alone

New road accident data from Maharashtra reveals that wrong-side driving on highways has killed 460 people in 2025 so far, establishing it as one of the most dangerous and persistent menaces on the state's road network. The figures underscore a systemic failure to enforce basic traffic rules on high-speed stretches, where wrong-way driving is frequently fatal. Maharashtra is one of India's most road-accident-prone states.

→ With 460 deaths in one state from a single, entirely preventable cause, the data makes a stark case for more aggressive enforcement and engineering solutions on Indian highways.

13:31 IST

Karnataka official, 26, killed in road accident; colleagues blame SIR workload pressure

A 26-year-old Karnataka government official has died in a road accident, with co-workers alleging that excessive workload related to the Socio-Economic and Educational Survey — commonly known as the SIR — contributed to the circumstances of the tragedy. The official's death has reignited concerns about the gruelling conditions imposed on government staff tasked with conducting the massive survey, which has been a political flashpoint in Karnataka.

→ The death highlights the human cost of large-scale government surveys conducted under pressure, and puts the Karnataka government on the defensive over the welfare of field-level officials.

23:24 IST

Chawl Collapses in Mumbai Amid Heavy Rain, 6 Killed Including 4 Women

A chawl collapsed in Mumbai amid heavy rainfall, killing six people including four women, with at least five others feared trapped in the rubble. The incident underscores the recurring danger posed by ageing, poorly maintained residential structures in Mumbai's dense neighbourhoods during the monsoon season. Rescue operations were launched immediately, with teams working through the night to pull out survivors.

→ Every monsoon season, Mumbai's crumbling chawls turn into death traps — this collapse, killing six and trapping more, is a brutal reminder of the city's unresolved housing safety crisis.
