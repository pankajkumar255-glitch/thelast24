The 6 stories that mattered, 05 Jul–12 Jul.

21:16 IST

Three Senior Ministers Fly to Brussels as India Accelerates EU Trade Mission

Three of India's top ministers have headed to Brussels on a trade mission aimed at advancing negotiations with the European Union, as Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal separately provided updates on the status of trade discussions with Canada, Israel, and Peru. The mission signals India's push to diversify and deepen its trade partnerships at a time of significant global economic realignment. The EU represents one of India's largest trading partners and a long-pending free trade agreement has been a priority for both sides.

→ Progress on an India-EU trade deal could unlock significant market access for Indian exporters in sectors from textiles to pharmaceuticals — directly affecting jobs and growth at home.

23:14 IST

Netanyahu Cites '1.4 Billion Indians' as He Pushes Back on Vance's 'US Is Israel's Only Ally' Claim

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu publicly pushed back against US Vice President JD Vance's assertion that the United States is Israel's "only ally", pointing to India's support and invoking "1.4 billion Indians" as evidence that Israel has friends beyond Washington. The exchange, which played out in international media, underscores the diplomatic significance of the India-Israel relationship. Netanyahu's remark is seen as both a rebuttal to Vance and an implicit acknowledgement of New Delhi's quietly steadfast position on Israel.

→ When the Israeli PM name-checks 1.4 billion Indians in a geopolitical argument, India's global diplomatic weight — and its relationship with Israel — is suddenly front and centre.

19:31 IST

Ukraine Accuses Russia of Burying 'Bioweapons' in Its Territory

Ukraine has accused Russia of burying what it describes as "bioweapons" within Ukrainian territory, calling the alleged activity a "ticking timebomb". The claim, reported by NDTV, represents a significant escalation in the information and accusations war running alongside the military conflict. Russia has not confirmed the claim, and independent verification is not available from the headlines provided.

→ Bioweapon allegations add a dangerous new layer to an already volatile conflict — one that global health bodies and India's own biosecurity planners will need to monitor.

13:52 IST

Khamenei Funeral: Three Sons Attend in Tehran as Successor Mojtaba Skips, US Pressure Kept Delegations Away

The funeral of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei was held in Tehran, attended by three of his sons, but notably absent was Mojtaba Khamenei, widely considered his likely successor. Reports indicate the United States pressured countries to skip or reduce their participation in the funeral, with at least 13 nations reportedly withdrawing or downgrading their delegations. The ceremony marks a pivotal moment in Iran's political future, with the question of succession now looming large.

→ Who leads Iran next will determine the country's nuclear posture, regional policy and relations with India — a major trading partner and energy supplier.

23:19 IST

Bilawal Bhutto Threatens India With War Over Indus Waters Treaty

Pakistan Peoples Party Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has openly threatened India with war, declaring Pakistan is 'ready on all fronts' in a direct escalation over the Indus Waters Treaty dispute. The statement comes after India moved to hold the treaty in abeyance following the Pahalgam terror attack, cutting off water-sharing commitments to Pakistan. Bilawal's remarks represent the sharpest public war threat from a senior Pakistani political figure in recent memory, ratcheting up already severe bilateral tensions.

→ A war threat from a former Pakistani foreign minister over the Indus Waters Treaty directly affects India's strategic calculus and the lives of millions dependent on those shared river waters.

20:49 IST

Trump Issues 'Finish The Job' Warning To Iran At Khamenei's Funeral

US President Donald Trump issued a stark warning to Iran as the country held funeral ceremonies for former Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, saying the United States would 'either make a deal, or finish the job.' The ultimatum delivered during the funeral period dramatically raises the stakes of ongoing US-Iran nuclear negotiations. Separately, funeral mourners were reported chanting threats against Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, with some offering rewards for their deaths.

→ A direct US military threat against Iran during a moment of national mourning — while India recently expressed condolences to Tehran — puts New Delhi's carefully balanced West Asia diplomacy under fresh pressure.
