Legendary Singer S Janaki, the Nightingale of South India, Dies at 88

S Janaki, one of the most celebrated playback singers in Indian cinema and widely known as the Nightingale of South India, passed away at the age of 88 in Mysuru due to age-related ailments. Her voice defined generations of Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam cinema, spanning a career of over six decades. Tributes poured in from across the film world, with luminaries including Rajinikanth, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay, and Kamal Haasan expressing grief at her passing.

→ S Janaki's voice was the soundtrack to the childhoods and memories of hundreds of millions of Indians across southern states — her death marks the end of a truly irreplaceable era in Indian music.

12:44 IST

Omi Vaidya and Naseeruddin Shah urge Sonam Wangchuk to end hunger strike

Actors Omi Vaidya and Naseeruddin Shah have publicly urged climate activist and engineer Sonam Wangchuk to end his hunger strike, with Vaidya invoking his 3 Idiots character by warning that 'Phunsuk Wangdu may die'. Wangchuk has been on a prolonged hunger strike pressing for constitutional safeguards for Ladakh. The appeal from film personalities adds a new dimension to the public pressure around Wangchuk's protest, which has drawn national attention.

→ Two prominent film voices stepping into the Wangchuk hunger-strike debate puts a Bollywood spotlight on Ladakh's statehood demands, potentially amplifying an issue that directly affects the constitutional future of a Union Territory.

17:20 IST

Sohail Khan Opens Up on Divorce from Seema Sajdeh: 'Wasn't in the Right Frame of Mind'

Actor Sohail Khan has spoken candidly about his divorce from Seema Sajdeh, admitting that he was not in the right frame of mind during the period that led to the breakdown of their marriage. The couple's split had played out partly in public view through the reality show that featured Seema Sajdeh, making their personal journey a subject of widespread discussion. Sohail's remarks are among the most direct he has made on the matter.

→ Bollywood separations rarely come with this level of personal candour from the quieter party — Sohail Khan's admission offers a rare, unfiltered look behind the curtain of a high-profile celebrity marriage.

16:00 IST

Anshula Kapoor Shares Unseen Wedding Reception Photos; Ranveer Singh and Arjun Kapoor Party Hard

Anshula Kapoor has shared a set of previously unseen photos from her wedding reception, giving fans a glimpse into the celebrations that followed her marriage. The pictures show Bollywood stars Ranveer Singh and Arjun Kapoor in high spirits, partying hard at the festivities. The images have quickly circulated on social media, drawing warmth from fans of the Kapoor family.

→ When Bollywood's inner circle lets its guard down at a family wedding, the candid moments become an event in themselves — and for fans, unseen photos from a star-studded reception are exactly that.

15:38 IST

Anjali Anand Says Her Role in 'Bell Bottom' Was Purely So 'a Terrorist Could Fall on Top of Me'

Actress Anjali Anand has made a sharp, self-deprecating observation about her role in the Akshay Kumar-starrer 'Bell Bottom', saying she was cast in the film only so that a terrorist could fall on top of her — pointedly critiquing the nature of the role she was given. The candid remark throws light on the often superficial parts that supporting actresses are offered in mainstream Bollywood action films.

→ Anjali Anand's blunt, witty critique cuts to a real and persistent issue in Hindi cinema — the tokenism of female roles in action blockbusters — and her willingness to name it publicly is notable.

08:16 IST

Dimple Kapadia's Granddaughter Naomika Saran Turns Heads in Black Saree at 'The Odyssey' Event

Naomika Saran, the granddaughter of veteran Bollywood actress Dimple Kapadia, made a stylish appearance at a premiere event for 'The Odyssey', wearing a black saree that drew widespread admiration. The appearance is being noted as much for her elegant sartorial choice as for her growing visibility as a young member of one of Bollywood's most enduring film families.

→ As the next generation of Bollywood's iconic families begins to step into the spotlight, Naomika Saran's public appearances are being watched closely as indicators of a possible screen career ahead.
